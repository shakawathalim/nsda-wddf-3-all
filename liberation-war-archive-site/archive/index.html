The record of a nation's birth.

The Liberation War Archive collects the documents, voices and photographs of Bangladesh's 1971 War of Liberation, so students, researchers and families can read, listen and look for themselves.

The war began on 26 March 1971 and ended with the surrender in Dhaka on 16 December 1971. Its records show how a people claimed their independence, and they should stay open to everyone.
