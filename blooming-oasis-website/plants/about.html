Our story

Nusrat bought her first monstera from a roadside nursery in Chattogram, killed it within a month, and bought another one out of stubbornness. By the end of the year her balcony was a jungle and her friends were asking which plant to buy and how to keep it alive.

She and her friend Tahmid started Blooming Oasis to answer those questions properly. Our mission is simple: bring nature indoors, and make every home a place where plants can thrive. We pick plants that suit real homes, we explain how to look after them, and we stay in touch long after the box has been opened.

Our mission

To bring nature indoors with healthy plants, honest advice and packaging that does not harm the planet.
