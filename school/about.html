History

Bright Future High School was founded in 1985 by a group of local educators who wanted every child in the neighbourhood to have a good school nearby. We started with two rooms and forty students.

A science lab was added in 1998, a library in 2005 and a computer lab in 2012. Today more than 1,200 students study with us from class six to class ten.

Head Master's Speech

A school is more than a building with classrooms. It is a place where children learn to ask questions, to work with others and to be kind. Our teachers work every day to make that happen, and I invite parents to be our partners in it.

Md. Golam Mostafa, Head Master

Mission and Vision

Mission

To give every student a strong education, good character and the confidence to keep learning after school.

Vision

To be a school our community is proud of, known for honest teaching, curiosity and care.
