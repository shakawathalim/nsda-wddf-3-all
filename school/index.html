Our History

Bright Future High School began in 1985 with two rooms, six teachers and forty students. Founded by a group of local educators, it grew year by year into a school of more than 1,200 students.

Today we are known for strong results in the secondary school certificate examination, an active library, and a culture of respect between teachers and students.
