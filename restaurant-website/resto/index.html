About Us

Saffron Table opened in 2014 with one idea: cook the food people grew up eating, and do it properly. Our recipes come from family kitchens across Bangladesh, and our spices are ground in-house every week.

Whether you are here for a quick lunch or a family dinner, we will find you a table.
